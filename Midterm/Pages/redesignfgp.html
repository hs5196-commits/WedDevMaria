Redesign Fort Greene Park

This project introduces a soft hydrological system into the rigid, heavy stairs to balance the park’s energy. By adding spongy rain gardens into the concrete landings, I transform a static barrier into an active, functional landscape. This design filters rainwater while providing new seating areas, turning the stairs into a vibrant social space for the community.

Project Goals

  • Rebalance: Connecting South (Active) to North (Static).
  • Manage: Using "Spongy" rain gardens for stormwater.
  • Activate: Turning rigid stairs into a social landscape.
