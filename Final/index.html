About Me

Urban Design & Architectural Stuides

Integrated Design Media | Teacher Education

I design spaces through observation, movement, and human experience. Rather than simply creating structures, I am interested in how people feel, interact, and behave within them. As a student in Urban Design and Architectural Studies at NYU, my work explores the relationship between urban environments and everyday life. I aim to create spaces that are not only functional, but also emotionally meaningful and engaging.
