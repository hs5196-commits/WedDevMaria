Playing Cards

This project applies Bauhaus methods by exploring form through simplification, abstraction, and material experimentation. Using playing cards as a starting point, elements are reduced and reorganized into fundamental shapes and compositions. It reflects a process of learning by making, where structure and visual balance emerge through iteration.

Project Goal

  • Apply: Using Bauhaus methods to explore form through simplification and abstraction.
  • Deconstruct: Breaking down playing cards into fundamental shapes and visual elements.
  • Recompose: Reorganizing these elements to create new compositions and spatial interpretations.
