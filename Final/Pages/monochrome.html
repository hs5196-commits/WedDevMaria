Monochrome Escape

My project, Monochrome Escape, explores the journey from a repetitive, grey-scale reality to a vibrant healing sanctuary. Inspired by the Wardrobe in The Chronicles of Narnia, a secret closet door serves as a portal that leads users into a glowing, pink Mureungdowon. In this surreal world, assets like the Dough Friend and Orbit Lantern guide the user, showing that a colorful space for emotional recovery is always just one door away.

Project Goal

  • Apply: Using the "Narnia" wardrobe concept to create a portal that bridges a rigid reality with a surreal healing dimension.
  • Deconstruct: Stripping away vibrant colors from a personal room to visualize the "grey burnout" of repetitive daily routines.
  • Recompose: Reorganizing emotional elements into a "Pink Mureungdowon" featuring soft-shaped avatars and glowing floating islands.
