Zig Zag Chair

This project began with the construction of a zig-zag chair, which was then reinterpreted through a series of collages. These explorations led to the development of a 3×3 cube, where the essence of the chair was translated into a spatial composition.

Project Goal

  • Construct: Building a zig-zag chair as a study of structure and form.
  • Interpret: Re-exploring the chair through collages to understand its spatial qualities.
  • Translate: Transforming the chair into a 3×3 cube as an abstract spatial composition.
